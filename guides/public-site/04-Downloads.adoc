
[#downloads]
== Downloads and Publications

_Downloads_ in the menu bar lists the publications of the authority: for example the weekly Notices
to Mariners, their annexes, a yearly almanac and other nautical publications. Each publication is
listed under the name of its category, with a book in front of its title.

The page has two tabs: _Active NtM and Publications_, with the publications that are current now,
and _Historical NtM and Publications_, with the ones of a period you choose.

[#active-publications]
=== Current publications

_Active NtM and Publications_ is the tab the page opens on. It lists the publications that are
public at this moment.

.Downloads: current publications
image::PublicSiteDownloadsActive.png[Downloads: current publications, 800]

Each publication has one or two links:

* _Download_ opens the publication in a new tab: its document, usually a PDF, which you can read,
  save or print there, or, for a publication another body publishes, that body's web page.
* _Browse_ is there for a publication made of messages, such as a weekly edition of the Notices to
  Mariners. It shows the messages of that edition on the site itself (see <<browse-publication>>).

[#historical-publications]
=== Older publications

_Historical NtM and Publications_ finds the publications of an earlier period.

.Downloads: older publications
image::PublicSiteDownloadsHistorical.png[Downloads: older publications, 800]

. Click the tab _Historical NtM and Publications_.
. Under _Date Interval_, choose the period: either click one of the year buttons, for this year and
  each of the four years before it, or fill in a date under _From_ and a date under _To_.
. The list shows the publications that were public at some time in that period. It appears when
  both dates are filled in, and changes when you change either of them.

The pressed year button shows which year the list is for. The dates are days in the time zone of
the authority, so a year runs from 1 January to 31 December there.

[#browse-publication]
=== Browsing a publication

_Browse_ opens the _Details_ view with the messages of that publication only, the way they were
published in it.

.Browsing a publication
image::PublicSiteBrowsePublication.png[Browsing a publication, 800]

While you browse a publication, the filter bar changes:

* The buttons _Navigational Warnings_, _Notices to Mariners_ and _Show Active Now_ are not there: the
  list is the publication's, whatever you had chosen before.
* _Print_ prints the publication's messages (see <<printing>>).
* A row of area buttons shows the sea areas the messages are in. Click an area to see only its
  messages; click more areas to add them, and click an area again to leave it out.

A publication may hold messages that have since been cancelled or have run out. A cancelled message
is marked _Cancelled_ beside its number, and one that has run out _Expired_, except a permanent
notice, which has no mark (see <<reading-a-message>>).

Nothing on the page names the publication you are browsing, so keep track of which one you opened.
The _List_ and _Map_ buttons of the menu bar, and _Details_ as well, take you back to the site's
current messages; to see the publication again, click _Browse_ on the _Downloads_ page once more.
