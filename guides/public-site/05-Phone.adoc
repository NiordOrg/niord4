
[#phone]
== On a Phone

The site works on a phone or a tablet the same way as on a computer, in the phone's own browser.
Nothing has to be installed. On a narrow screen a few things look different.

[#phone-messages]
=== The menu bar and the messages

.The Details view on a phone
image::PublicSiteDetailsPagePhone.png[The Details view on a phone, 244]

* The menu bar shows only the symbols of the four views, without their names: a list beside boxes
  for _Details_, a list for _List_, a globe for _Map_ and a downward arrow for _Downloads_. The flags
  of the languages follow them.
* The buttons of the filter bar take two rows.
* In a message, the name of each part (_Details_, _Charts_ and so on) stands above its text instead
  of beside it.
* A table of positions that is wider than the screen keeps each position on one line, and scrolls
  sideways inside the message: swipe the table to see the rest of it.

[#phone-message-window]
=== A message's window

A message's window takes most of the screen, with the map at the top and the text under it. Scroll
the window to read the whole message; the buttons are at the bottom.

.A message on a phone
image::PublicSiteMessageDialogPhone.png[A message on a phone, 244]

[#phone-map]
=== The map

On a phone the panel _Additional Messages_ lies along the top of the map, beside the zoom buttons
_+_ and _−_, which stay free, and it leaves the credits at the bottom of the map showing. Click
_Minimize_ to fold it up to its title; _Maximize_ opens it again. Move the map with one finger and
zoom with two, or with the zoom buttons. The picture shows navigational warnings only, each with a
position, so the panel is not there.

.The Map view on a phone
image::PublicSiteMapPagePhone.png[The Map view on a phone, 244]

On a phone there is no mouse pointer to rest on a symbol: tap the symbol to open its message.
