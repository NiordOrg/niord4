
[#map]
== The Map

[#map-view]
=== The Map view

_Map_ in the menu bar shows the messages on a map: the same messages as the _Details_ and _List_
views, the ones you chose in the filter bar. When it opens, the map is placed so that every message
on it is in view.

.The Map view
image::PublicSiteMapPage.png[The Map view, 800]

To move around the map:

* Drag the map to move it.
* Zoom in and out with the _+_ and _−_ buttons in the top left corner, with the wheel of the mouse,
  or with two fingers on a touch screen.

The map is drawn from OpenStreetMap, whose credit is in the bottom right corner. It is not a nautical
chart: use it to find the messages, and your charts to navigate.

[#map-symbols]
=== What the symbols mean

Every message is drawn in magenta, in the way an electronic chart draws navigational warnings, and
carries the letters of its kind: _NW_ for a Navigational Warning, _NM_ for a Notice to Mariners.

.Points, lines and areas on the map
image::PublicSiteMapSymbols.png["Points, lines and areas on the map", 800]

[cols="30,70",options="header"]
|===
|What you see |What it is
|A circle with _NW_ or _NM_ in it
|A message about one position, such as a light or a buoy, or about a few positions, one circle for
each.
|A dashed line with boxed _NW_ or _NM_ tags along it
|A message about a line, such as a cable, a pipeline or a channel. The tags follow the line, so you
see what it is wherever you look along it.
|A dashed outline with one boxed _NW_ or _NM_ tag inside
|A message about an area, such as a work area or a firing area. The area is not filled in, so you
can see the map under it.
|A paler dashed outline around a message
|The area around a position or a line that the message says is affected, for example the distance
to keep from a vessel at work.
|===

Where many messages lie close together, such as in a harbour, their symbols overlap. Zoom in to tell
them apart, or point at them (see <<map-pointing>>).

[#map-pointing]
=== Pointing at and opening a message

Rest the mouse pointer on a symbol, or inside an area, to see which messages are there: a small box
names up to three of them, each with its number and title. When there are more, the box ends with
the number of the others, for example _and 9 more messages_.

.Pointing at a message on the map
image::PublicSiteMapTooltip.png[Pointing at a message on the map, 255]

Click a symbol, or inside an area, to open the message in its window (see <<message-window>>). Where
several messages lie at the same place, the click opens the first one the box names; the arrow
buttons of the window then step through the messages of the map in the order of the list.

[#additional-messages]
=== Messages without a position

Some messages have no position to draw, for example a notice to all ships in a country's waters.
The Map view lists them in a panel called _Additional Messages_, in the top right corner of the map,
with their numbers and titles. Click one to open it in its window.

_Minimize_ folds the panel up to its title, so that it leaves the map free; _Maximize_ opens it
again.

[#message-map]
=== The map of one message

The window of a message with positions has a map of its own, above the text, which shows that
message alone (see the picture in <<message-window>>).

* The map shows the message's symbols, and places a numbered circle on each position. The numbers
  are the same as in the position tables of the text, so you can find position 3) of the text on
  the map.
* When a position has a name in the text, such as the name of a buoy or a light, the name is
  written under its circle.
* _Labels_, in the bottom left corner, hides the numbers and the names, and shows them again.

Some sites can also show a sea chart on the map of a message. They then have a second box in the
bottom left corner, _Sea Chart_, which shows and hides it.
