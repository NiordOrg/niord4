[#firing-schedules]
== Firing Schedules

Firing areas are the sea areas where the armed forces hold firing exercises, and mariners are warned
of each exercise. Niord writes these warnings by itself: editors keep a schedule of the times each
firing area is in use, the _firing periods_, and Niord turns the periods into _firing exercise_
messages. A _firing schedule_ is the set-up that does this: it names the domain that holds the
firing areas' messages and the domain and message series that receive the firing exercise messages.

The _Firing Schedules_ page lists the firing schedules. To open it, click *Sysadmin* in the main
menu, then *Firing Schedules*.

image::FiringSchedulesPage.png[The Firing Schedules page with its action menu open, 800]

The columns are *Active*, the *Domain* of the firing area messages, the *Target Domain* and the
*Target Series* of the firing exercise messages. The *Filter* field matches the two domains' names
and the target series. The action menu (the cog) has *Update firing exercises...* (see
<<generating-firing-exercises,Generating Firing Exercises>>); firing schedules have no import or
export.

[#firing-exercises-system-setup]
=== Firing Exercises System Setup

Before a firing schedule can do its work, these parts must be in place. The editors' side, the
_Schedule_ page where they enter the firing periods, is described in the
link:../editor-manual/manual.html#firing-exercise-schedule[Firing Area Schedule] chapter of the
Editors Manual.

. *The firing areas.* Each firing area is an area of Niord's base data with the type _Firing area_,
  defined by an administrator on the Admin
  link:../admin-manual/manual.html#areas[Areas] page.
. *A domain for the firing areas*, such as _Firing Areas_, with a message series for notices to
  mariners. It holds one published message for each firing area, with the firing area among the
  message's areas, which describes the area: its limits, the signals, whom to contact, what is
  prohibited. Editors keep these _firing area messages_ up to date like any other message.
. *A domain for the firing exercises*, such as _Firing Exercises_, with a message series of its own,
  normally for local navigational warnings. Its messages are written by Niord, so no editor should
  have the right to edit messages in it.
. *The firing schedule*, which ties the two domains together (below). The domain of the firing
  areas then has the _Schedule_ page in its main menu.

[#configuring-firing-area-base-data]
==== Configuring Firing Area Base Data

image::FiringArea.png[A firing area on the Areas page, 800]

An administrator defines every firing area of the waters on the Admin
link:../admin-manual/manual.html#areas[Areas] page, as an area with the *Type* _Firing area_. Only
areas of that type have firing periods, and only active ones are scheduled.

[#create-a-firing-areas-domain]
==== Create a Firing Areas Domain

The domain of the firing areas holds the firing area messages, one for each area:

image::FiringAreaMessages.png[The firing area messages of the Firing Areas domain, 800]

An administrator can have Niord draft a firing area message for every active firing area with
*Generate firing area messages…* in the action menu of the Admin Areas page; editors then complete
and publish them. The drafts get the category whose English name is "Firing Exercises", so keep that
category and its name (see <<categories,Categories>>). The list of these messages is also a natural basis for a yearly publication of the
firing areas; see the link:../admin-manual/manual.html#publications[Publications] chapter of the
Administrators Manual.

[#maintain-firing-exercise-schedule]
==== Maintain Firing Exercise Schedule

The editors enter the firing periods of each area, day by day, on the _Schedule_ page of the domain
of the firing areas, which they open with *Schedule* in the main menu. The page is described in the
link:../editor-manual/manual.html#firing-exercise-schedule[Firing Area Schedule] chapter of the
Editors Manual. It is there only in a domain that is the *Domain* of a firing schedule.

[#generating-firing-exercises]
==== Generating Firing Exercises

Every hour, at 21 minutes and 50 seconds past the hour, Niord goes through every firing schedule
that has *Generate firing exercises* ticked:

. It takes the active firing areas of the schedule's *Domain*, and the published messages of that
  domain's series that have one of the firing areas among their areas.
. It takes the firing periods of those areas from today to the number of days ahead the schedule's
  *Scheduled Days* says.
. For each firing area message with firing periods, it writes a firing exercise message in the
  *Target Message Series*: a local warning (or, for a series of notices to mariners, a temporary
  notice) with the firing area message's areas, categories, charts, texts and positions, the firing
  periods as its event dates and as a time text written in the target domain's time zone, and the
  subject "Firing exercises. Warning.". It is published at once and expires after its last firing
  period.
. A firing exercise message that has the same firing periods as before is kept. When the periods of
  an area have changed, Niord publishes a new message and cancels the old one, and it cancels the
  message of an area that has no firing periods left.

The time text and the subject come from the "message" dictionary (the entries
`msg.time.date_time_format`, `msg.time.time_format` and `msg.firing_exercises.subject`; see
<<dictionaries,Dictionaries>>).

*Update firing exercises...* in the action menu of the Firing Schedules page runs the same pass at
once, for every active firing schedule, without asking first. Editors have the same action on the
_Schedule_ page.

[#adding-or-editing-a-firing-schedule]
=== Adding or Editing a Firing Schedule

Click *New Firing Schedule* to add a schedule, or the pencil of a row (*Edit Schedule*) to edit one.
Both open the editor drawer:

image::FiringScheduleEditPage.png[The Edit Firing Schedule drawer, 324]

[cols="30,70", options="header"]
|===
| Field
| Description

| Domain
| The domain of the firing area messages. It gets the _Schedule_ page. A new schedule starts with
  the current domain.

| Target Domain
| The domain the firing exercise messages are written in.

| Target Message Series
| The message series of the firing exercise messages, one of the target domain's series. When the
  target domain has one series only, it is chosen for you.

| Scheduled Days
| Optional. How many days ahead the firing exercise messages cover. Left empty, the number is the
  setting `firingExerciseScheduleDays`, 30 by default (see <<settings,Settings>>).

| Generate firing exercises
| Ticked, the schedule writes firing exercise messages (the _Active_ column of the list).
|===

The three domain and series fields are required, and their lists offer the domains in which you hold
a role. Click *Save Firing Schedule* to save.

[#deleting-a-firing-schedule]
=== Deleting a Firing Schedule

Click the bin of a row (*Delete Schedule*) and confirm with *Yes*. Niord stops writing firing
exercise messages for the schedule, and its domain loses the _Schedule_ page. The firing exercise
messages already published stay until they expire. To pause a schedule instead, untick *Generate
firing exercises*.
