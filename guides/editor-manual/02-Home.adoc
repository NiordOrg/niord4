[#home]
== Home

The _Home_ page is the page the administration application of Niord opens with. It shows the
published messages of the current domain on a map that fills the window, with a small welcome
panel on top of it: the Niord logo, _Welcome to the Nautical Information Directory_ and, while you
have not logged in, a _Login_ button. Point at a message on the map to see its title. You come back
to the _Home_ page from anywhere by clicking the Niord logo at the left of the header.

image::Home.png[Home, 800]

Everyone can open the _Home_ page and the _Messages_ page, also without logging in: you then see
the public messages (published, cancelled and expired) and can read them, but you cannot change
anything. The pages for editors,
administrators and system administrators ask you to log in first.

This chapter describes what every page has in common: the header, the help, logging in and out,
and choosing the domain and the language.

[#application-header]
=== The Header

The header runs across the top of every page. From left to right it holds:

* The Niord logo, which takes you to the _Home_ page.
* The menu items _Messages_, _Editor_, _Schedule_, _Admin_ and _Sysadmin_. _Messages_ is always
  there. Each of the others appears only when your role in the current domain lets you open the
  page behind it: _Editor_ for an editor, an administrator or a system administrator, _Admin_ for an
  administrator or a system administrator, and _Sysadmin_ for a system administrator. _Schedule_
  appears to the same roles as _Editor_, and only in a domain that has a firing schedule.
* At the right: the help (a question mark), the domain menu (the name of the current domain with a
  coloured disc), your account menu (a person icon; before you log in, the _Login_ button, an arrow
  pointing into a door) and the language menu (a flag).

On a narrow window, such as a tablet held upright, the menu items fold into one menu button. It
opens a single list with the pages, the help, your account and the languages. The domain menu
stays visible beside the button, because it tells you which data you are working on.

A deployment that is not the production system, for example a test system, shows a red badge
under the logo, such as _TEST_, so that nobody mistakes test data for real warnings and notices:

image::DeploymentBadge.png[Badge of a Test System, 49]

[#help]
=== Help

The question mark in the header, image:HeaderHelpLink.png[Help, 16], opens the section of the user
guides that describes the page you are on, in a new browser tab. For the _Home_, _Messages_ and
_Editor_ pages that is a section of this manual; the _Schedule_, _Admin_ and _Sysadmin_ pages open
the manuals for administrators and system administrators. A page that no section describes shows
no question mark. Many dialogs and panels have a question mark of their own next to their title,
which opens the section about that dialog.

The address of the manuals is a setting that a system administrator can change, so a deployment
can point the help at its own copy of the manuals.

[#login]
=== Logging In and Out

Before you log in for the first time, ask a Niord administrator for a user account and for the
roles you need in each domain. Your roles can differ from one domain to another: you may be an
editor in one domain and only able to read in another.

To log in, click _Login_ in the header or in the welcome panel on the _Home_ page. Niord takes you
to the login page of its sign-in service. Enter your username and password and click _Sign In_.
Once you are logged in, you come back to the page you were on.

image::Log-In.png[Login, 350]

The login page is set up for each deployment. Depending on the set-up, it may also offer your
organisation's own login, or ask for a one-time code after the password.

When you are logged in, the person icon in the header is your account menu. Point at it to see your
name. The menu has two items:

* _Edit <your name>_ opens your account pages in the sign-in service, where you change your
  password and your profile.
* _Logout_ logs you out.

image::ProfileMenu.png[Account Menu, 167]

[#session-expired]
==== When Your Session Expires

If your session expires, or the sign-in service cannot be reached, Niord does not log you out or
reload the page. It carries on as if you had not logged in and shows a strip under the header,
_Your session has expired — you're browsing as a guest._ or _Login is currently unavailable —
you're browsing as a guest._, with a _Login_ button:

image::GuestBanner.png[Guest Banner, 800]

Click _Login_ to log in again. Logging in or
out loads the application afresh: your message selection is emptied, and if the editor holds
changes you have not saved, the browser first asks whether you want to leave the page.

[#domain-selection]
=== Choosing the Domain

As described in the <<editors-manual,Editors Manual>> section, you always work within a current
_domain_ in Niord. The current domain is chosen in the domain menu in the header, which shows its
name and a disc in the domain's colour:

image::DomainMenu.png[Domain Menu, 167]

Next to each domain name, a pencil shows that you hold a role in that domain, and an eye that you
can only view its published messages. The current domain is shown in bold on a grey background.

Choosing another domain loads the application afresh, at the same address in most cases. Your
message selection is emptied. If the editor holds changes you have not saved, the browser first
asks whether you want to leave the page, and the changes are lost if you go on, so save your work
before you switch.

The browser remembers your choice, so Niord opens in the same domain the next time. A link to Niord
that ends in `?domain=` followed by the id of a domain opens Niord in that domain.

[#language-selection]
=== Choosing the Language

The language menu at the right of the header chooses the language of the menus, buttons and
labels. The languages it lists are set up for each deployment: the base product comes with English
only, and a country adds its own languages. Lists of messages show each message in the chosen
language when the message has a text in it.

As with the domain, the browser remembers your choice for the next time.

[#out-of-date-warning]
=== When Niord Is Updated

[[update-banner]]Niord notices by itself when a newer version has been installed while you have it
open: when you come back to the browser tab, or when a page you open cannot be loaded any more. It
then shows a banner under the header on whatever page you are on, _Please refresh web-browser_,
with a _Retry_ button:

image::UpdateBanner.png[Update Banner, 800]

Click _Retry_ in the banner to load the new version. Save your work in the editor first.

After the button, the banner names in small print the _Server Version_ and the _Web Version_, the
versions of the two parts of Niord; the picture leaves them out. Give them when you report a problem.
