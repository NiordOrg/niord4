
[#mariners]
== For Mariners Using the Public Site

The public site is where mariners and anyone else read the Navigational Warnings and Notices to
Mariners without signing in. It has been replaced by a new site. It shows the same messages, but it
looks different, and a few things work differently. The base product's site is in English; a
country's site adds its own languages, which you choose with the language buttons in the menu bar.

[#mariners-reading]
=== Reading the Messages

[#mariners-views]
==== Details, List and Map

The menu bar has three views of the messages. _Details_, where the site opens, shows the full text
of every message under the heading of its area. _List_ shows one line for each message, with its
number and title. _Map_ shows the messages on a chart, and lists the messages that have no position
in a panel called _Additional Messages_.

[#mariners-filter]
==== Choosing what is shown

Above the messages, _Navigational Warnings_ and _Notices to Mariners_ switch each kind of message on
or off; one of them is always on. _Show Active Now_ limits the list to the messages in force at
this moment. _Print_ opens the messages you are looking at as a PDF document, ready to print or
save.

[#mariners-one-message]
==== One message, and links to it

Clicking a message in the _List_ or on the _Map_ opens it in a window with its map, its full text,
arrows to the previous and next message, and a button that prints that message alone. A single
message also has an address of its own, `/messages/details/message/` followed by its id, which you
can bookmark or send to someone.

[#mariners-addresses]
==== Addresses without the "#"

The addresses of the old public site had a `#` in them, for example `/#/messages/map`. The new
site's addresses have none: the map is simply `/messages/map`. Bookmarks and links written for the
old site still work, because the new site turns them into the new form when it opens.

[#mariners-publications]
=== Publications

[#mariners-downloads]
==== The Downloads page

_Downloads_ in the menu bar lists the publications, such as the weekly Notices to Mariners, grouped
by category. Its first tab, _Active NtM and Publications_, holds the publications that are current
now. The second, _Historical NtM and Publications_, holds older ones: choose a period under _Date
Interval_ to see what was published in it.

[#mariners-browse]
==== Download or browse an issue

A publication with a document has a _Download_ link, which opens it. A publication made from
messages also has _Browse_, which shows the messages of that issue on the _Details_ view of the site, so you
can read them there instead of in the document.

[#mariners-what-is-listed]
==== Why a publication is or is not listed

A publication is listed when three things are all true: its category is one the site shows, it has
not been withdrawn, and the period in which it is public has begun. A withdrawn publication
disappears from the site, from the historical tab as well, but messages that cite it still show
the reference.

[#mariners-other-sites]
=== For Sites That Show the Messages

[#mariners-teaser]
==== The teaser for other web sites

Another web site can show a small map of the current messages, the _teaser_, which leads to the
public site when it is clicked. Its address is `/teaser`, and it accepts `area` to limit it to one
area and `lang` to choose the language. A teaser address written in the old form, with `#?` before
these parameters, still works.

[#mariners-phones]
==== On a phone

The site works on a phone. On a narrow screen the menu bar shows its icons without their labels,
and the panel of messages without a position can be made smaller so that it leaves the map free.
