The Union Flag, 64x64 with transparent margins: the flag at 60x40 from (2,12), the size and place
the language packs' flags share (niord-lang-da, niord-lang-kl). Drawn for Niord from
img/flags/en.svg in this pack.

Construction: the Flag Institute's Union Flag specification
(https://www.flaginstitute.org/wp/uk-flags/union-flag-specification/), in units of the hoist,
which is 30. St George's cross, red, 6 wide with a white border of 2 each side. The diagonals 6
wide each, St Andrew's white saltire, carrying St Patrick's red saltire counterchanged: white 3,
red 2, white 1, the broad white uppermost at the hoist and the narrow white uppermost at the fly.
Each diagonal runs corner to corner and the two meet in the centre; widths are measured square to
the diagonal. Colours as the specification gives them: blue Pantone 280 C, drawn #012169; red
Pantone 186 C, drawn #C8102E; white, which it leaves open, #FFFFFF.

Where it departs: the specification's field is 30 by 50 (3:5) on land and 30 by 60 (1:2) at sea.
Here the field is 30 by 45 (3:2), so that the flag fills the 60x40 box as the other packs' flags
do. The cross and the diagonals keep the specification's widths, and the diagonals run
corner to corner of the 3:2 field. The SVG's coordinates are rounded to a thousandth of a unit.

with rsvg-convert 2.60.0-r0 in Alpine 3.22, the image pinned by its digest and run without a
network, twice, both runs giving the same bytes (sha256
f522d224b2174e11de54807153a16a57867ad17121980f021d9fc53e323c8258):

  FROM alpine:3.22@sha256:5291449c3df73caf6ed85e649dec1b9e818b39a5d8c871e97afc13e9cd5e8fa8
  RUN apk add --no-cache rsvg-convert=2.60.0-r0

  docker run --rm --network none -v "$PWD:/w" <that image> \
    rsvg-convert --width 64 --height 64 --format png --output /w/en.png /w/en-64.svg
